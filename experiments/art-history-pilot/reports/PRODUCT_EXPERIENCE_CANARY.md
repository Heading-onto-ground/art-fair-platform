# PRODUCT EXPERIENCE CANARY

Phase P2-B. Isolated static prototype. Production SEO, routes, and analytics were not changed.

Status: **PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE_READY_FOR_HUMAN_TEST**

This is a technical readiness verdict. It is not a finding that the experience is compelling. That question needs a person.

## 1. What was built

`experiments/art-history-pilot/view-v2/index.html`, generated by `scripts/build-view-v2.ts` from the accepted pilot records and the frozen seed. The earlier checkpoint `view/index.html` is still there.

The screen is English-first. The Korean name sits under the English name. Search matches the English name, the Korean name, and verified aliases from the seed and from a confirmed Wikidata identity. Unresolved identities do not show a birth year.

There is no follow button, no follower count, no likes, and no feed. HISTORY is the only working view. CONNECTIONS, WORKS, and ABOUT are labels in the hierarchy, not separate pages. "Are you an artist? Start your history →" explains that first-party creation is not open in this pilot.

## 2. Artists and data

The five densest accepted histories are Lee Ufan (130), Park Seo-Bo (87), Lee Bae (67), Haegue Yang (58), and Ha Chong-Hyun (52). Search also lists the rest of the International 25.

Lee Bul is the empty-state artist: 0 accepted exhibitions. The page says "No accepted exhibition records in ROB yet."

History Signature bars are counts of accepted years. Open circles on the timeline are year-only dates. A day-precision date would be a filled circle. The current dense histories are mostly year-only, so most circles are open.

## 3. Desktop behavior

A horizontal line runs from the earliest recorded year to the latest. Dots sit on that line. Year labels mark the span. The page was opened in a desktop browser: search, Haegue Yang's timeline from 2000 to 2026, and the year-only legend all rendered.

## 4. Mobile behavior

At a 390px viewport the horizontal track is hidden and a vertical list is shown. Each row has a year, a title, and a venue. This was checked on Lee Ufan. The first row is 1967, Sato Gallery, Tokyo. The layout is a separate list, not a shrunk copy of the horizontal track.

## 5. Timeline behavior

Dots and rows use the stored date precision. Year-only records are not given a fake month. The legend says "Open circles mark year-only dates."

## 6. Moment expansion

Choosing an exhibition opens that exhibition only: title, date and precision, venue, city when the stored city is short, curators if any, other frozen artists named on that row, and the source links. No inferred related artists are added. Curators are absent because the accepted records contain none.

## 7. Connection navigation

Park Seo-Bo's 2025 exhibition "The Making of Modern Korean Art" names Lee Ufan. Opening it shows a Lee Ufan control. Choosing it replaces the timeline with Lee Ufan and shows "You came here through The Making of Modern Korean Art…". Back returns to Park Seo-Bo. The path uses the stored artist names. It does not say they are friends or closely connected.

## 8. Source UX

The same moment shows "Sources 2" and two links, both Kukje artist pages (`seq=190` and `seq=181`). Those are the two official pages that were merged onto one exhibition. Long page text is not copied into the layout.

## 9. Empty state

Lee Bul's page has her name, 이불, birth year 1964, a count of 0, and the sentence "No accepted exhibition records in ROB yet." No exhibitions were added to fill it.

## 10. Local interaction logging

Events stay in `localStorage` under `rob-art-history-pilot-log`. The page can clear the log and export it as JSON. A check of resource requests while using the page found no request to an analytics host. The logged types exercised were SEARCH_PERFORMED, ARTIST_OPENED, TIMELINE_EVENT_OPENED, SECOND_ARTIST_REACHED, CONNECTION_FOLLOWED, and RETURNED_TO_PREVIOUS_ARTIST. SOURCE_OPENED is logged when a source link is clicked. The export uses a local blob URL.

## 11. SEO-preview semantics

The document title becomes `{Artist}: Exhibition History & Connections | ROB`. The description becomes "Explore {Artist}'s documented exhibitions, spaces, curators and artist connections through time." This happens only inside the local file. No production route, sitemap, or robots change was made.

Experimental density buckets, not a production rule:

| Bucket | Rule | International artists |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| EMPTY | 0 | 8 |
| LOW_DENSITY | 1–7 | 8 |
| HISTORY_READY | 8–29 | 4 |
| RICH_HISTORY | 30 or more | 5 |

The principle under test is that an empty or thin entity page should not be mass-indexed. EMPTY and LOW_DENSITY are the artists who would stay out of a future sitemap. The numbers are not a launch rule.

## 12. Known limitations

Many Kukje and Johyun rows use the gallery name as the title, so early timeline rows repeat the venue. Three titles still contain HTML entities. One venue still includes a copyright line. Desktop dots overlap where several exhibitions share a year. CONNECTIONS, WORKS, and ABOUT do not open their own views. A co-participant is clickable only when that person is a frozen artist with accepted records. Cities longer than 40 characters are hidden in the layout and left unchanged in the data.

Yes, as a prototype. Search leads to a history, a history opens one documented moment, that moment can lead to another frozen artist, and Back returns. Whether a person wants to keep going is not answered here.
